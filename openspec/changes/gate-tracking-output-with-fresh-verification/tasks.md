# Tasks

All boxes are implementation work, not claims of completion. Follow [design.md](design.md) and the four capability deltas: `tracking-output-validity`, `detection-tracking-arbitration`, `target-observations`, and `rk3576-tracking-regression`. Changes to controller or bridge repositories, mission commands, manual annotation, and calibrated score/motion rejection are outside this checklist.

The reopened boxes cover separate seed eligibility, removal of no-result frame-count cleanup, and one-call detector scheduling with regional result semantics. One asynchronous YOLO worker and take/submit/arbitrate ordering remain; indivisible cascades and first-class-positive confirmation do not. Dated reports describe their original snapshots and do not establish completion of this revised work. Temporary test profiles do not change the formal defaults.
